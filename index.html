Hi, I’m Melanie 👋

I am a PhD student at the TUM Chair of Computational Imaging and AI in Medicine and the Munich Center for Machine Learning (MCML), working on 3D reconstruction from medical ultrasound imaging data.

I hold an M.Sc. in Mathematics in Data Science from the Technical University of Munich (TUM). I’m passionate about bridging the gap between mathematical theory and real-world applications.

Outside of academia, I enjoy building hands-on projects like Meveta, a dictation and documentation software for equine veterinarians, or posEQuest, a computer-vision based coaching tool for equestrian sports.
